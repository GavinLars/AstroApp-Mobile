Atlas values estimate artificial brightness at zenith. They are not Bortle scores, field measurements, or guarantees of on-site conditions.

Atlas: David Lorenz, World Atlas of Artificial Night Sky Brightness (2025). North America detail uses a 1/120° grid; the world view uses a coarser 1/40° grid. Grid spacing describes map detail, not prediction accuracy. Local lighting, terrain, atmosphere, and horizon glow can differ.

Use AstroApp offline

  1. Open this app address in Safari while online.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then turn on Open as Web App.
  3. Open the Home Screen app once and wait for Offline ready.
  4. Switch on Airplane Mode and reopen it to confirm your setup.

Sky calculations, maps, star data, and gear are stored locally. The five tabs make no online requests during use.
